# Candidates

**Winner (111 characters):**
My phone only charges if the cable is bent at one exact angle, like I'm cracking a safe every night before bed.

**Runner-up (137 characters):**
My voice assistant never understands the one command I say every day, yet it can hear a whispered mention of shoes from three rooms away.

## Why the winner won

Fourteen candidates were drafted — lying battery percentages, update prompts, autocorrect, a flashlight app begging for five stars in the dark, password rules, a fitness watch keeping secrets about cake — and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria: funniness, freshness of angle, and neatness of wording. The runner-up's ad-listening twist is fresh and pointed, but it needs a beat to set up; the winner lands in a single read. Its image — cracking a safe every night just to charge a phone — is one everyone has physically lived, it is not one of the angles named in the bounty brief itself, and at 111 characters every word earns its place.
